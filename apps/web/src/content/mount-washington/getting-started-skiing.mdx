Mount Washington Alpine Resort offers some of the best skiing on Vancouver Island. Whether you're a complete beginner or just new to the mountain, this guide will help you make the most of your first day on the slopes.

## What to Expect

Mount Washington receives an average of 11 meters of snow annually, making it one of the snowiest ski resorts in North America. The resort features:

- 60 runs across 505 acres
- 7 lifts including a high-speed quad chair
- A vertical drop of 505 meters
- Terrain for all skill levels

## Beginner Runs

If you're just starting out, head to the Whiskey Jack area. The green runs here are perfect for learning the basics:

- **Easy Street** - A gentle groomed run that's perfect for your first attempts
- **Sunrise** - Slightly longer but still beginner-friendly
- **Boulevard** - Wide open spaces to practice your turns

## Rental Equipment

The resort has a full rental shop at the base lodge. We recommend arriving early (before 9 AM) to avoid the rush. You'll need:

- Skis or snowboard
- Boots
- Poles (for skiers)
- Helmet (highly recommended)

## Lessons

First-time skiers should absolutely take a lesson. The ski school offers:

- Group lessons (more affordable, social)
- Private lessons (personalized attention)
- Multi-day packages (best value for learning)

Book lessons in advance, especially during peak season (December-February).

## What to Bring

- **Warm layers** - It's colder at the summit than at the base
- **Waterproof gloves** - Essential for staying comfortable
- **Sunscreen & sunglasses** - The sun reflects off the snow
- **Snacks & water** - Stay hydrated and energized
- **Change of clothes** - For after skiing

## Getting There

Mount Washington is about a 2-hour drive from Nanaimo. Check road conditions before you go and ensure your vehicle has winter tires. The resort provides regular updates on snow conditions and road status.
