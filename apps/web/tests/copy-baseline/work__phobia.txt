onboarding process Previously users selected their phobias manually but this led to some users selecting things they weren’t actually afraid of to increase their matches To combat this we developed a volume and duration of their screams and feed that information to the matching algorithm The next phase is a VR version of the onboarding flow where users are subjected to a series of scenarios that
